📖 PixelBear's Dungeon Crawler — Game Guide

🎯 Goal

Guide a party of up to 6 adventurers through an endless dungeon. Explore, fight monsters, collect treasure, and take the stairs (>) ever deeper. Every 5th floor is a boss floor. The game ends when the whole party falls.

🚀 Quick start

  1. Type each player's name at the top of their sheet and pick a class from the dropdown.
  2. The GM may enter starting points per player in Party Setup; spend them by left-clicking stats on the sheet (right-click reclaims).
  3. Pick Difficulty and Formation in Party Setup (locked after the first move).
  4. Tick each player's Ready box to lock them in. When all are ready, move to begin!

⚔ Combat system

Each class has three abilities, on the 1 / 2 / 3 keys or the purple buttons in Player Options:

ClericMace Blow 1d6, meleeHealing Word 2d4, range 2 — heals most wounded ally (or self)Divine Smite 2d8 radiant (3-turn cd)

Damage formula: dice + stat modifier ((stat − 10) ÷ 2) + level bonus (+1 per 2 levels) + Power Items (+2 each). Spending attribute points on your class's key stat directly raises your damage. Every roll is written to the Adventure Log and echoed in the Dice Roller panel.

Spell charges: 2 + level÷3 + casting-stat modifier. Restored by Resting or descending stairs. Power attacks recharge after their cooldown, shown under the ability buttons.

Walking into a monster also attacks it (basic melee). Monsters strike back when attacked in melee, and nearby allies give +2 damage each (max +6) and improve your defense.

🧪 Items & status effects

  • 🧪 Healing Potion — restores 20 HP • 📜 Scroll of Blessing — +2 Atk, 5 turns • ⚡ Potion of Haste — +2 movement, 4 turns • 🍃 Antidote — cures poison • 🗝 Iron Key — opens a locked door
  • Poison ☠ damage each turn • Stun blocks acting • Bless +Atk • Haste +movement • Slow −movement. The active sheet's border tints with the effect color.

🎲 Dice roller

Under Player Options: pick dice count, type (d4–d%), modifier, then Roll — or Adv/Dis for advantage/disadvantage d20s. Rolls are cryptographically random, logged to the Adventure Log, shared to multiplayer chat, and natural 20/1 highlighted. The game's own ability rolls appear here too.

💀 Death & difficulty

  • Easy / Normal: downed members may make a death save (30% to cling on at 25% HP). Shrines Θ and Clerics can revive the fallen.
  • Hard / Nightmare: permadeath — the fallen stay dead (remove them with the × at the bottom of their sheet). More and stronger monsters, less XP.

📈 Leveling

XP comes from exploring (3 XP the first time you step on a tile; pacing back and forth earns nothing), kills (10–25 + Wis bonus), and Power Items (60). Each level: +2 to stats you invested starting points in, +1 every second level to the rest, full heal, higher max HP/Atk/Def. XP needed grows with level and shrinks with party size.

💾 Saving

Esc menu → pick a save slot name → Save / Load / Delete. Loading warns before replacing your current game. Saves live in your browser's local storage.
